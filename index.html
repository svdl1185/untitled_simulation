A complete ocean

The aim is the most complete coupled ocean that can run in a browser — every kind of ocean, not one pretty tank. A North Sea shelf, a Humboldt upwelling cell, an Antarctic slope, a tropical gyre, a coral reef, an ice edge, a hydrothermal vent: each kilometre should be a living system, not a backdrop.

Physics first, then chemistry, then life. Light, nutrients, oxygen, and the seafloor set the budgets; animals only exist if they eat something and are limited by something. The camera is a window. If a feature does not change a budget, a habitat, or a sensory cue, it does not belong yet.

The system at once

Glossary

PAR
Photosynthetically active radiation. The light phytoplankton can use. Dies with depth (Beer–Lambert). Fog, caustics, growth, and visual hunt share it.
Kd
Diffuse attenuation. How fast PAR dies with depth (turbidity). Sets the 1% light depth — the photic limit.
NPZD
Nutrients, phytoplankton, zooplankton, detritus. The bloom budget. N, P, Z, D. A 128×128 patch times a shared column, not a 128³ grid.
DVM
Diel vertical migration. Night near the surface to feed; day deeper as a visual refuge. Zooplankton, forage fish, lanternfish, and Humboldt squid all run a schedule.
OMZ
Oxygen minimum zone. A hypoxic band below the mixed layer in eastern-boundary and tropical cells. Humboldt’s day refuge. Tunas and sharks stay above their o2Min.
DSL
Deep scattering layer. The mesopelagic sound and biomass layer. Lanternfish in this catalog; photophores restore a fraction of visual detect in the dark.
Q10
How much a rate changes per 10 °C. Metabolism, graze, and NPZD all scale with column temperature.
SST
Sea-surface temperature. Latitude climatology plus season plus the anomaly knob. Gates some catalog rows.
Occupancy
Seasonal presence on a hull. The day-of-year slider. Not a swim between cells.
Mixed layer
Well-mixed surface water. Winter mixes it deeper; storms mix it deeper still. Shear, oxygen, and NPZD all read that depth.
Nutricline
The depth where nutrients increase. Climate upwell and storms shoal it so N sits in the light.
Photic
The sunlit layer, down to ~1% light. Photosynthesis lives here. Below it the window goes near-black, not herring-green.
Ice
Sea-ice concentration and thickness from latitude and season. Pack ice shades PAR; ice algae produces in the top metres. Polar night / midnight sun follow the sun.
Benthos
Organic carbon on the seafloor (pelagic rain plus microphytobenthos) and living infauna density. Cod graze the living store.
MPB
Microphytobenthos. Light-driven production on photic floors — the shelf analog of ice algae.
Type II
Saturating graze: fast when food is scarce, capped when it is dense. School fish on z or p; filters on the bloom.
GEBCO / GMRT / HYCOM
Atlas. GEBCO tints the world map; GMRT is the seafloor under the animal; HYCOM is a mean current when that service answers.
Vehicle
A rare Reynolds integrator — sharks, whales, giant squid, air-breathers. Abundance is a catalog knob: numerous mesopredators are hashed-grid school with diet: "bite", not a second 20k loop.
Agents / Eulerian
Near the camera: individuals. Kilometres out would be density. The basin is fields on a grid. Not more fish farther out.

Coupled now

  • Cell and atlas. World map is home. Click water for a 1 km cell (GEBCO tint, GMRT floor, HYCOM mean current when it answers). Land is not a cell. Cells is a picker of named kilometres: the 2 × 2 km catalog tank (every species, stepped shelves, 2000 m) plus biomes from the coupling figure. Overlay paints current habitat for any catalog taxon — several at once — on this day of year (range vs spawn, search, a day-of-year scrubber). Gap tiles still open the pelagic water at that site — they do not invent coral or a vent. Polar ice and a living bed are coupled. Neighbour chunks cache; animals do not swim between cells. Clock: 16 min ≈ 1 day, twelve sim days per year. Polar night follows the sun. Storms mix deeper, shoal the nutricline, and raise current.
  • Fields. One shared current (sampleFlow: tide, longshore, shear, eddies, atlas mean, climate upwell). Beer–Lambert PAR (samplePAR) shared by fog, caustics, phytoplankton, and visual hunt. Pack ice is an I0 skin. A shelf floor inside the photic envelope stays sunlit. Temperature and Q10 (sampleTemp). Dissolved oxygen and OMZ (sampleO2). Sea ice (climateIce: concentration, thickness, ice algae). Separable NPZD (128×128 patch × column: N, P, Z, D). Benthos: detritus and microphytobenthos onto the bed; living infauna; cod graze the living store.
  • Agents. One hashed-grid school (cap 20k): grazers on a bloom cap, piscivores on a prey-capped slice of the same grid. Vehicles are the rares and air-breathers. Silhouettes are authored glTFs: one mesh per vehicle (orca male/female dorsal), one guild mesh per school look.shape, recolored from catalog look. Social modes: polarized, loose, scatter. Vehicles that live in groups travel as pods (orca one matriline; common dolphin 1–3 pods; hammerhead and bluefin one school). Gaits on vehicles: burst, ram, jet. Air-breathers swim to the surface before the tank is empty, hang and blow, then a flukes-up dive. Empty tank underwater is drowning. Spawn against the local seafloor; maxDepth is biological, then the floor, then oxygen.
  • Budgets. Grazer ceiling = bloom mean × photic production. Piscivore ceiling = live forage / prey ratio on the same grid. Type II graze on z or p. Bites restore energy; missing prey is a starve. Sperm whale × giant squid is the vehicle-on-vehicle bite; Humboldt is school huntTaxa. Q10 and hypoxia scale rates. Closed mass: graze, excretion, carcass, sink, remineralisation, MPB uptake, infauna graze, benthos graze.
  • Range. Hulls, Wikipedia / IUCN / Cypron rasters, and catalog niches scored by season, kilometres from shore, SST, ice, OMZ, upwell, and shelf-vs-oceanic floor, then trophic gates. A matching Wikipedia world map is scanned onto a 360×170 occupancy raster (scripts/wiki_ranges.json); family/genus ids do not steal a single-species map. Neritic taxa without a scanned map clip a hull envelope to kilometres from shore, so Overlay is a coastal ribbon, not a filled box. Occupancy scales abundance on a year-round hull; it does not empty a cell the species still lives in. School share and vehicle count scale with the weight. Overlap is habitat. Empty is honest.
  • Window. Water, fog, and fish follow the same Kd. Depth layers, station briefing for this kilometre, census, field-notes cards, physical knobs (turbidity, SST anomaly, oxygen anomaly, ice anomaly, day of year, storm). Map Overlay paints live habitat (range vs spawn). Camera lamp (K) is a dive torch — a local beam, not a scene fill, and not habitat.
