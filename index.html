A complete ocean

The aim is the most complete coupled ocean that can run in a browser — every kind of ocean, not one pretty tank. A North Sea shelf, a Humboldt upwelling cell, an Antarctic slope, a tropical gyre, a coral reef, an ice edge, a hydrothermal vent: each kilometre should be a living system, not a backdrop.

Physics first, then chemistry, then life. Light, nutrients, oxygen, and the seafloor set the budgets; animals only exist if they eat something and are limited by something. The camera is a window. If a feature does not change a budget, a habitat, or a sensory cue, it does not belong yet.

The system at once

Glossary

PAR
Photosynthetically active radiation. The light phytoplankton can use. Dies with depth (Beer–Lambert). Fog, caustics, growth, and visual hunt share it.
Kd
Diffuse attenuation. How fast PAR dies with depth (turbidity). Sets the 1% light depth — the photic limit.
NPZD
Nutrients, phytoplankton, zooplankton, detritus. The bloom budget. N, P, Z, D. A 128×128 patch times a shared column, not a 128³ grid.
DVM
Diel vertical migration. Night near the surface to feed; day deeper as a visual refuge. Zooplankton, forage fish, lanternfish, and Humboldt squid all run a schedule.
OMZ
Oxygen minimum zone. A hypoxic band below the mixed layer in eastern-boundary and tropical cells. Humboldt’s day refuge. Tunas and sharks stay above their o2Min.
DSL
Deep scattering layer. The mesopelagic sound and biomass layer. Lanternfish in this catalog; photophores restore a fraction of visual detect in the dark.
Q10
How much a rate changes per 10 °C. Metabolism, graze, and NPZD all scale with column temperature.
SST
Sea-surface temperature. Latitude climatology plus season plus the anomaly knob. Gates some catalog rows.
Mixed layer
Well-mixed surface water. Winter mixes it deeper; storms mix it deeper still. Shear, oxygen, and NPZD all read that depth.
Nutricline
The depth where nutrients increase. Climate upwell and storms shoal it so N sits in the light.
Photic
The sunlit layer, down to ~1% light. Photosynthesis lives here. Below it the window goes near-black, not herring-green.
Benthos
Carbon on the seafloor from sinking detritus. Cod graze that field. The animals of the bed are not agents yet.
Type II
Saturating graze: fast when food is scarce, capped when it is dense. School fish on z or p; filters on the bloom.
GEBCO / GMRT / HYCOM
Atlas. GEBCO tints the world map; GMRT is the seafloor under the animal; HYCOM is a mean current when that service answers.
Vehicle
A handful of Reynolds integrators — sharks, tunas, whales, squid, cod — not a second 20k boid loop. The hashed grid is the school.
Agents / Eulerian
Near the camera: individuals. Kilometres out would be density. The basin is fields on a grid. Not more fish farther out.

Coupled now

  • Cell and atlas. World map is home. Click water for a 1 km cell (GEBCO tint, GMRT floor, HYCOM mean current when it answers). Land is not a cell. Cells is a picker of named kilometres: the 10 km catalog tank (every species, stepped shelves, 2000 m) plus biomes from the coupling figure. Gap tiles still open the pelagic water at that site — they do not invent coral or ice. Neighbour chunks cache; animals do not swim between cells. Clock: 8 min ≈ 1 day, twelve sim days per year. Storms mix deeper, shoal the nutricline, and raise current.
  • Fields. One shared current (sampleFlow: tide, longshore, shear, eddies, atlas mean, climate upwell). Beer–Lambert PAR (samplePAR) shared by fog, caustics, phytoplankton, and visual hunt. Temperature and Q10 (sampleTemp). Dissolved oxygen and OMZ (sampleO2). Separable NPZD (128×128 patch × column: N, P, Z, D). Benthos: detritus flux onto the bed.
  • Agents. One hashed-grid school (cap 20k) plus a handful of vehicles. Social modes: polarized, loose, scatter. Gaits: burst, ram, benthic, jet. Air-breathers hang and blow, then a flukes-up dive. Spawn against the local seafloor; maxDepth is biological, then the floor, then oxygen.
  • Budgets. School ceiling = bloom mean × photic production. Type II graze on z or p. Bites restore energy; missing prey is a starve. Sperm whale × Humboldt / giant squid are the vehicle-on-vehicle bites. Q10 and hypoxia scale rates. Closed mass: graze, excretion, carcass, sink, remineralisation, benthos graze.
  • Range. Hulls OR; trophic, thermal, OMZ, and shelf gates. Overlap is habitat. Empty is honest.
  • Window. Water, fog, and fish follow the same Kd. Depth layers, census, field-notes cards, physical knobs (turbidity, SST anomaly, oxygen anomaly, storm). Camera lamp is fill, not habitat.
